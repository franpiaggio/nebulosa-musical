Who plays what

Only the big stars, the ones with a diffraction cross, make sound, and their height picks what: with Harmonics, a high star plays a higher note; with Piano chords, near the top the arc's opening chord and near the bottom its resolution. A low D pedal holds everything together. Add spiked stars in the Stars tab to write more notes. One sweep of the line is one arc of the sequence, about 27 seconds. Play and pause with the button under the image or the space bar. Add or erase stars in the Stars tab to change the melody.
